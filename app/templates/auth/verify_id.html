Before taking your picture

  1. Have your original ID document ready.
  2. Place the ID on a flat surface.
  3. Make sure the entire ID is visible.
  4. Make sure you have enough lighting.
  5. Avoid glare and reflections on the ID.
  6. Make sure all text is clearly visible.
  7. Keep your phone steady when taking the picture.
  8. Allow camera access when your browser asks for permission.
